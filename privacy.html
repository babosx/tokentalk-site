TokenTalk Privacy Policy

Effective 2026-10-21

TokenTalk is an Android app for families doing ABA and early-words practice at home. It is made by an independent developer (Babos).

What data the app stores

Child names, pictures you choose or take, rewards, routines, and practice results. All of it is stored only on your device, inside the app's private storage.

What leaves your device

Nothing, unless you choose to share it. The app has no internet permission, no accounts, no analytics, no ads, and no third-party SDKs that collect data. The only way data leaves the device is when you tap Export and pick an app to share the CSV or PDF with; that share goes to the app you choose, not to us.

Backups

If you have Android's own backup turned on, your TokenTalk data (including photos you added) is included in your device's Google backup, under Google's terms. You can exclude the app in Android's backup settings.

Children

TokenTalk is operated by a parent or caregiver. It does not collect personal information from children, and it does not transmit any information about them.

Deleting your data

Use Parent settings > Erase everything, or uninstall the app. Both remove all stored data from the device.
